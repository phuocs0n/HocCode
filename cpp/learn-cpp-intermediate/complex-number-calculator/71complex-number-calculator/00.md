#### Revision: Complex Numbers

We know that complex numbers are composed of two parts: a real part consisting of a real number and an imaginary part consisting of an imaginary number. For example, consider the complex number given below:

```cpp
5 + 7i
```

Here,

- 5 is the real part
- 7i is the imaginary part, where i = √-1

Now, let's move on to performing arithmetic operations on complex numbers.
