## Example: Arithmetic Assignment

### Example 1

```cpp
1#include <iostream>
2using namespace std;
3
4int main() {
5
6    int total = 100;
7 
8    // total = total + 10 
9    total += 10;
10
11    cout << total;
12
13    return 0;
14}
15
16// Output: 110
```

Here, total += 10; adds 10 to the total variable and assigns the result back to the total variable.

### Example 2

```cpp
1#include <iostream>
2using namespace std;
3
4int main() {
5
6    int x = 100;
7  
8    // x = x % 9
9    x %= 9;
10
11    cout << x;
12
13    return 0;
14}
15
16// Output: 1
```

Here, x %= 9 computes the remainder when x is divided by 9 and assigns the result back to x.
