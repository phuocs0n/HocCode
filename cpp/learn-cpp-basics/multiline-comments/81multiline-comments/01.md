## Example: Multi-line Comments

```cpp
1/*
2Program to take age input
3from the user and print it
4*/
5
6#include <iostream>
7using namespace std;
8
9int main() {
10
11    int age;
12
13    cout << "Enter your age: ";
14    cin >> age;
15
16    cout << "Age: " << age;
17
18    return 0;
19}
20
21/* Output:
22Enter your age: 56
23Age: 56
24*/
```

Just like single-line comments, multi-line comments are also ignored during the execution of the program.

Multi-line comments are generally used to write long-form instructions and documentation.
