## Introduction

A heap is a special type of tree where each node has a key that is either greater, less, or equal to the key of its parent. In other words, if a particular key in the heap is greater than its parent, all other key too will be greater than their parent.

This property is called the heap property.

There are two types of heap property:

1. Max-Heap Property
2. Min-Heap Property
