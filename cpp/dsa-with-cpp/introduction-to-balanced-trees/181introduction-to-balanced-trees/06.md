## Why Balance Trees?

Balancing a tree guarantees two primary benefits:

1. Predictable Performance: With a balanced tree, our operations run at a consistent and fast speed.
2. Space-Efficient: We don't waste memory due to excessive depth.

This means that basic operations on our balanced tree take a predictable amount of time, usually denoted as O(logn), where n is the number of nodes.
