## Working of Divide-And-Conquer Algorithm

The divide-and-conquer approach involves three main steps:

1. Divide: Divide the given problem into smaller problems using recursion (divide vector into subvectors of one element).
2. Conquer: Solve the smaller subproblems recursively. Our subvector consists of only one element, which is already sorted (no action required).
3. Combine: Combine subsequent subproblems recursively to solve the actual problem (individual subvectors are combined to form a sorted vector).
