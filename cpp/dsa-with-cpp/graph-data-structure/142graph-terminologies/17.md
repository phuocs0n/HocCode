## Graph Representation

Let's take a moment to revise what we have learned so far.

We started by defining graphs as a collection of interconnected vertices and also came up with a formal definition of a graph as G(V, E).

We've understood them through diagrams; learned about their directed, undirected, and weighted types; and delved into the relationships between their elements.

However, we cannot rely on diagrams in programming. Instead, we need mathematical models to represent graphs. In our course, we will focus on two primary representations:

- Adjacency Matrix
- Adjacency List

In the next lesson, we'll look at adjacency matrices and see how we can implement them in C++.
