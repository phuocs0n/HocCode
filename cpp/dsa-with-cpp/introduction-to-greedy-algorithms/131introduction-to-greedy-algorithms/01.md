## Principles of Greedy Algorithms

There are three things to note about greedy algorithms:

1. Short-Sighted: Greedy algorithms choose the best option at the moment without thinking about the consequences in the future.
2. No Looking Back: Once a decision is made, greedy algorithms don't change their minds, even if it proves to be a suboptimal choice in the end.
3. Iterative Approach: Greedy algorithms solve problems step by step, always choosing what looks best right now.
