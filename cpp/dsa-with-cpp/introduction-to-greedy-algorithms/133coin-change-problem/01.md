## Defining the Greedy Logic

The greedy approach to solving this problem has the following steps involved.

1. Start by choosing the largest coin denomination that can be used without exceeding the amount needed.
2. Now, update the amount to be paid by subtracting the value of the coin just used.
3. Repeat this process until the amount becomes zero.
