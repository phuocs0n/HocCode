## Thought Process

To separate even and odd numbers in an array, determine if the numbers inside the array are even or odd and add them to their respective arraylist.

Let's look at a detailed overview of how to solve this problem.

Suppose we are given a numbers array.

- Create two integer arraylists named evenNumbers and oddNumbers to add even and odd numbers, respectively.
- Loop through each element of the numbers array.
- Check whether the number is even or odd and add that number to its respective arraylist.
