## Getting the Most Out of This Course 🚀

You'll get the most value from this course if you treat it like a real project, not something to rush through.

As you go, try this.

- Take a moment to study the design before coding.
- Try things, even when you're not 100 percent sure.
- Expect to tweak and adjust your code.
- Compare what you built with what you see.
- Celebrate small improvements.

Frontend progress can feel invisible day to day. But every layout decision, every spacing fix, every cleanup compounds. By the end, you'll feel that difference clearly.
