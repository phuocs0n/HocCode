## You Have the Foundation

This course was intentionally focused. Responsive design is a deep topic. There's clamp(), srcset, container queries, CSS Grid intrinsic sizing, and more. We didn't cover all of it and that was the point.

The goal was to give you enough to understand the concept and fix a real broken website. Not a complete reference. A working foundation. The kind where you can open DevTools, resize to 320px, and know exactly what to look for.

Everything beyond this builds on what you already know.
