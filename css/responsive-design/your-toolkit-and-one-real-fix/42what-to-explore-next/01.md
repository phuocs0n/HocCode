## Fluid Typography with clamp()

Right now your h1 has a fixed font size. It looks great on desktop but can feel too large on a 320px phone.

clamp() lets a value scale smoothly between a minimum and maximum based on the viewport width. No media query needed.

```css
h1 {
  font-size: clamp(1.75rem, 5vw + 1rem, 3.75rem);
}
```

On a small screen it's at least 1.75rem. On a large screen it's at most 3.75rem. In between it scales fluidly. One line replaces a media query.
