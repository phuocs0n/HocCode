## Responsive Images with srcset

max-width: 100% stops images from overflowing but the browser still downloads the full size file.

A 1200px portrait on a 320px phone downloads all 1200px worth of data even though it only displays at 140px.

srcset lets you provide multiple image files at different sizes. The browser picks the right one.

```css
<img
  src="portrait-400.png"
  srcset="portrait-400.png 400w, portrait-800.png 800w"
  sizes="(min-width: 48rem) 14rem, 100vw"
  alt="Portrait of Abidit"
/>
```
