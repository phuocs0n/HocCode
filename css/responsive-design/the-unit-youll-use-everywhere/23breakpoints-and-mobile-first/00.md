## What Is a Breakpoint?

You just wrote your first media query. You picked 48rem as the width where the hero switches from two columns to one. That width, the moment your layout needs to change, is called a breakpoint.

It's not a magic number. It's just the moment your content needs more space.

Think about your portfolio step by step. On a phone around 360px everything is a single column, the nav stacks, the hero stacks, the cards stack.

That's the base. At around 768px, a tablet, there's enough room for the hero to open up into two columns. At around 1024px, a desktop, the stat cards can spread into four columns.

Each of those moments is a breakpoint.
