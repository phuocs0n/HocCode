## One Layout Isn't Enough

Right now your portfolio looks the same at every width. 360px, 768px, 1440px. Same layout, same columns, same everything.

Think about it. A phone is narrow. There's no room for a portrait image and a block of text sitting side by side. But on a tablet or desktop there's plenty of space for exactly that.

What you really want is a way to say "stack everything on a phone, but show two columns on a wider screen."

That's exactly what media queries do.
