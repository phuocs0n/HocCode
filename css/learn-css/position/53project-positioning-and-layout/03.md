## Step 3: Logo Positioning

Next, let's focus on positioning the logo with these CSS:

- Change the logo's font size and float it to the left.
- Apply some padding to center the nav items.

```html
1<body>
2    <nav class="navbar">
3        <div class="logo">GreatBrand</div>
4        <div class="navbar-items">
5            <div class="navbar-item">About</div>
6            <div class="navbar-item offer">
7                Special Offers
8                <span class="offer-badge">New!</span>
9            </div>
10        </div>
11    </nav>
12    <main>
13        <p>This is a sample navbar.</p>
14    </main>
15</body>
```

```css
1.navbar {
2    color: white;
3    background: #111111;
4    height: 70px;
5    position: sticky;
6    top: 0;
7
8    /* Padding added */
9    padding: 20px;
10}
11
12.logo {
13    font-size: 20px;
14
15    /* Logo floated left */
16    float: left;
17}
```
