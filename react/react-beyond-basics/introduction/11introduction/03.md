## The Gap: What Real Apps Also Do

Open any app you use every day — a news reader, a to-do list, a journaling tool — and you'll notice a few things.

1. The URL changes as you navigate.

If you're on an entry detail screen, the address bar says something like /entries/42. You can copy that URL and send it to someone, and they'll land on the same screen.

2. The browser's back button also works.

Press it and you go back to where you were previously, not to a blank screen.

3. Settings persist as you move between pages.

The settings and themes you choose — like dark mode — aren't lost when you navigate.

4. The app doesn't load everything at once.

Pages you haven't visited yet don't download until you actually navigate to them.

But here's the problem: our current app does none of these things!

- Everything lives inside one URL (/).
- There's no shared state across multiple views.
- State management logic is spread directly in components.
- The entire bundle loads upfront.

This course bridges that gap.
