## Revisiting the Changes

Here's what changed from the previous lesson:

- A new Drawer component was added. It renders the full entry details and closes on × click or outside click.
- App gained a selectedEntry state and a handleSelectEntry() function. Clicking a card sets selectedEntry. Closing the drawer resets it to null.
- EntryCard gained an onSelect prop. Clicking the card calls onSelect() with the entry data.
