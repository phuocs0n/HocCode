## Connect Build to Personal Brand

Every build you ship publicly should connect back to something you are known for - or want to be known for. A build post that is purely "look what I made" misses the opportunity to reinforce your professional identity.

For SpeedBlip, the connection was: I teach structured development methodology. The post did not just share the app - it shared the process. "Built in one session using a 10-step methodology" told my network that this was not just a project - it was a demonstration of how I think about building software.

Ask yourself before writing your launch post: what does this build say about how I work? What professional belief does it demonstrate? The most effective build posts are not "I built X" - they are "I built X and here is the principle behind it."

This also creates reusable content. The SpeedBlip post naturally led to follow-up posts about prompt architecture, brand-first building, and the Framer AI workflow - each of which had its own reach because they connected to the original narrative.
