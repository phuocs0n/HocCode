## Engage in the First Hour

Publishing the post is the beginning, not the end. The first hour after publishing is when LinkedIn's algorithm is watching most closely. Every comment you respond to increases the post's engagement rate and signals that it is worth showing to more people.

What I did in the first hour after the SpeedBlip post:

- Responded to every comment within 5 minutes of it appearing
- Asked follow-up questions in my replies to extend the conversation thread
- Replied to the first comment (with the SpeedBlip link) to make it visible in notifications
- Did not edit the post after publishing (editing resets algorithm momentum)

One comment thread where I asked "what speed did you get?" led to 12 replies as people compared speeds. That single thread accounted for roughly 30% of the post's total engagement.

The effort investment is about 20-30 minutes of active monitoring after posting. For a piece of content that can reach thousands of people who would not otherwise see your work, it is worth every minute.
