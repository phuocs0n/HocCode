## Naming Your Product

A good product name does one job: it sticks. It does not need to be clever. It does not need to be a real word. It needs to be short, easy to say, and ideally something that hints at what the product does.

SpeedBlip came together fast because it follows a simple pattern: it combines a descriptor (Speed) with a short, punchy word that evokes the product behavior (Blip - as in a small signal, a reading on a monitor). Together they imply "a speed signal" or "a quick speed reading."

Here is what I look for when naming a product:

- Short - ideally 2 syllables or one compound word
- Pronounceable - if you cannot say it out loud without thinking, it is too hard
- Available - check the .com and the social handles before you commit
- Evocative - it should hint at what the product does, even loosely

You do not need a perfect name before building. But you do need a name you will not want to change mid-build - because renaming a product after you have written 40 component files is painful.
