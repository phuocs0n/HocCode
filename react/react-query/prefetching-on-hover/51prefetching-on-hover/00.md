## Why Prefetching Feels Like Magic

You know that feeling when you click something and it loads instantly? Like there was no wait at all?

That's usually prefetching. The app quietly fetched the data before you even clicked. It did it the moment you hovered, because hovering usually means you're about to click.

In TaskFlow when the user hovers over a task we can silently fetch that task's details in the background. By the time they click it's already loaded.
