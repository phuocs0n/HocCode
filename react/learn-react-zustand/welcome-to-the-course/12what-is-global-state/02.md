## When It Gets Out of Hand

Now imagine your journal app looks like this.

```react
App
├── Header
├── JournalForm
└── EntryList
```

Your entries live in App. But JournalForm needs to add entries. EntryList needs to display them. Header needs them for the search feature.

So you start passing them down as props.

For a small app it is manageable. But imagine your app grows into this:

```react
App
└── Layout
    └── PageWrapper
        └── MainContent
            └── ContentArea
                ├── Sidebar
                │   └── FilterPanel
                │       └── SearchBar
                └── Feed
                    └── EntryList
                        └── EntryCard
```

Every single component in that tree has to accept the props and pass them down. Layout does not care about entries. PageWrapper does not care.

ContentArea does not care. Sidebar does not care. They are all just passing the message along.

And every time you add something new to your state, you have to touch every single one of them — even the ones that have nothing to do with it.

The bigger the app, the deeper the drilling. The deeper the drilling, the harder it is to follow where your data is actually going.
