## Why Zustand?

Here is what makes Zustand a great fit, especially when you are learning.

No special wrapper needed

Some tools require you to wrap your entire app in a special component before they work. Zustand does not. You create a store and use it anywhere, immediately.

No extra syntax to learn

Your state and the functions that change it all live in one plain JavaScript object. If you know JavaScript objects and functions, you already understand most of Zustand.

Tiny

The whole library is less than 1KB. It adds almost nothing to your app.

Smart re-renders

Components only update when the exact piece of state they use changes. Not everything. Just their piece.

Simple. Lightweight. Stays out of your way.
