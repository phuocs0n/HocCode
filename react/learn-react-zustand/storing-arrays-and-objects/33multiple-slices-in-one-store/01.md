## Your Store is Growing

Look at what is in your store now.

```react
entries: [],
isLoading: false,
error: null,
theme: 'light',
```

State for entries. State for loading. State for errors. State for the theme. Different concerns living in the same place.

That is fine for now. But as the store grows it helps to think of it in groups — or slices. A slice is just a section of your store that belongs to one feature. No special Zustand API needed. You just organize your state and actions by feature.

Next, you will see how to think about slices in your journal store. Click Next Lesson to continue.
