## The Day My Todo List Ate Itself

This is a story from the infant days of my developer journey. I had a simple task in front of me: make a simple todo list. Just HTML, a bit of CSS, and some JavaScript.

"Sounds easy enough!", I thought to myself.

Three days later, I had 47 lines of document.getElementById, innerHTML, and addEventListener scattered throughout my JS file.

Every new feature I added broke something else. I'd a "mark complete" button, and the delete button would stop working. I then fixed delete, only to find the completed items lost their styling.

My code was basically a house of cards, and I was playing whack-a-mole with the DOM.

Then I discovered React, and my project finally started working.

So what exactly is React? Let's explore that a little bit.
