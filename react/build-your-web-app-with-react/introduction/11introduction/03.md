#### Course: Build your First Web App with React

## Meet Your Mentor

I'm Aatish Shakya, a senior software developer with 4+ years of hands-on experience building real products with React.

Now some of you might think that I graduated out of my engineering degree with full knowledge of React, but the truth is that I didn't (almost no one does!).

In fact, I started building pages with plain JavaScript: manually grabbing DOM elements, and wondering why my UI kept getting out of sync.

When I picked up React, a lot of tutorials skipped the "why" and jumped straight into the syntax. That made it harder than it needed to be.

So in this course, I always show the problem before the solution.

By the time a new concept appears, you'll already feel why you need it.

Next, I'll lay out some of my expectations for you as a learner, and I believe following them will enable you to learn React fast and effectively.
