## How to Learn Effectively

Here's what I expect from you:

- Read the code, don't just watch it. Trace through every example line by line, and tinker with it whenever possible.
- Run the practice problems now, not later. The moment you skip one, the concept stops sticking.
- Be okay with confusions. Getting stuck isn't a sign you're bad at this; it's a sign you're learning.

And that's enough of my expectations.
