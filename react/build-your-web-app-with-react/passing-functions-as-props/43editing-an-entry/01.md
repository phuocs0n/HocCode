## The Problem: A Saved Entry Can't Be Edited

Right now users can add and delete entries. But they cannot fix a typo or update what they wrote.

So adding an Edit button is a no-brainer. The whole process may look something like this:

1. You click Edit on a card.
2. Then, you change the title or content.
3. Finally, you click Save.
4. The updated text must then replace the old entry in the state.
