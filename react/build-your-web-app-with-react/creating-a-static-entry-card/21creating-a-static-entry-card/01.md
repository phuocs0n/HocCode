## Add the Entry Card

Below the header, you'll be adding a <div> with a title and some text content:

```undefined
1function App() {
2  return (
3    <div>
4      <header>
5        <h2>My Daily Journal</h2>
6        <p>Capture your thoughts, one entry at a time</p>

8      <div>
9        <h2>First Day at the Job</h2>
10        <p>Today was my first day at the new job. Everyone was welcoming and helpful. I'm excited to learn more about the team and start contributing.</p>
11      </div>
12    </div>
13  );
14}
15
16export default App;
```

Click Run Code. You should see the header and the entry card below it in the output.
