## Why This Breaks User Experience

Imagine using an app that freezes every time it needs data.

You click Load Articles and the entire page becomes unresponsive. You can't scroll. You can't click other buttons. You can't even close the browser tab easily.

This makes the app look completely broken. Users won't know if the app is still working or if it has crashed, leaving them unsure whether to wait or refresh the page.

That's a horrible user experience for your webpage, and a headache for the user.
