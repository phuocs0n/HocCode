## Meet Your Instructor

Hi, I'm Aatish 👋 and I've been a professional developer with over six years of experience working with JavaScript.

Like many developers, I didn't struggle with async JavaScript because of syntax.

I struggled because the async and runtime concepts didn't make sense at first.

That confusion is exactly why this course is structured the way it is. The way I wish async had been taught to me, with:

- Real-world examples
- Honest explanations about what is confusing and why

Throughout the course, I'll occasionally point out:

- Common misunderstandings
- Mistakes developers often make early on
- Better ways to think about async behavior

The goal is not perfection, but clarity and confidence.

So, let's get started!
