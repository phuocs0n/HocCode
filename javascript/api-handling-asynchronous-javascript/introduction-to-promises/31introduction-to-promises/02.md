## Promise as a Real World Object

Imagine you're in a coffee shop during the morning rush and you're there for that bitter (yet refreshing) liquid the shop serves. Just one cup is enough to make your day.

So you get in line and it's finally your turn to order. Here's what happens:

- You order your coffee.
- But you don't get the coffee immediately.
- Instead, what you get is a receipt.

That receipt:

1. Exists immediately
2. Represents your future coffee
3. Lets you track your order

You don't wait doing nothing: you keep the receipt and decide what to do when the coffee arrives.

A promise is that receipt, and it works the same way.

When an async operation starts:

- The work begins
- You don't get the result immediately
- You get a promise instead.

That promise represents a future result.
