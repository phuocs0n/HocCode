## Numbers

The two common types of numbers in JavaScript are:

#### 1. Integers

Integers are numbers without decimal points. They can be positive, negative, or zero.

Examples: 5, -11, 0, 12

#### 2. Floating-Point Numbers

Floating-point numbers contain decimal points. Like integers, they can also be both positive, negative, or zero.

Examples: 2.5, 6.76, 2.1, -9.45
