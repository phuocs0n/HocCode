## Displaying Multiple Values

You can display multiple pieces of data at once by separating them with commas:

```javascript
1// Display two numbers
2console.log(67, -5, 19);
3
4// Display two strings
5console.log("Hey.", "Are you enjoying the course?");
6
7// Display numbers and strings
8console.log("Rating:", 8.5);
```

Output

```text
67 -5 19
Hey. Are you enjoying the course?
Rating: 8.5
```

Here, the comma is used for separation and is not printed.

Additionally, spaces before or after the comma don't affect the output. By default, console.log() always inserts a single space between the values.
