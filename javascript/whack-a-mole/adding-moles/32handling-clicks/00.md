## Time to React! 🖱️

Great progress! In the last lesson, you made moles pop up from holes. But right now, clicking them does nothing.

That's about to change.

This lesson is all about making your game respond to player actions. You'll learn how to detect when a player clicks a mole and make something happen—like making the mole disappear.

By the end of this lesson, clicking a mole will:

- Remove the mole from view
- Feel responsive and game-like
- Set you up perfectly for tracking score in the next lesson

Let's make your game interactive!
