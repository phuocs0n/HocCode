## Random Chaos! 🎲

This is it—the final piece that makes your game feel like a real game!

Right now, only one mole appears (the first one, hardcoded for testing). That's not much of a challenge. Real Whack-a-Mole is unpredictable—moles pop up randomly from different holes, and you never know which one is coming next.

In this lesson, you'll add that randomness. Moles will:

- Appear from random holes
- Pop up automatically at regular intervals
- Disappear after a short time
- Keep the player on their toes!

This is the most complex lesson in the chapter, but you'll build it step by step. By the end, you'll have a fully functional, playable game!

Let's make it exciting!
