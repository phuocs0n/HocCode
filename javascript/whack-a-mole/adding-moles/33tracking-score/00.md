## Keeping Count! 🎯

Great work! Your moles can now pop up and disappear when clicked. But there's one crucial thing missing: the score.

Right now, your game has no way to track how many moles the player has hit. Every game needs feedback—players want to know how well they're doing!

In this lesson, you'll add score tracking. Every time a player clicks a mole, the score will increase and update on the screen.

By the end of this lesson:

- Clicking a mole will add 1 point
- The score will display on screen
- Your game will feel more complete and rewarding

Let's make hits count!
