## What is a Promise?

A Promise in JavaScript is just like a promise in real life.

For instance, you go to a restaurant and order pizza. When you order pizza, the waiter takes your order. It is like the waiter promising to give you pizza after some time.

Now, until you see the waiter come with the pizza, the promise is pending.

After some time, the waiter comes with the pizza, which means he has fulfilled the promise.

However, there might be a case where there is no pizza dough left, so the waiter comes and tells you he can't give you pizza. The promise has been rejected.

So, here are the three cases:

1. Pending: Right after the promise is made and you are waiting for the pizza.
2. Fulfilled: When you get the pizza.
3. Rejected: In case there is no dough left and you don't get the pizza.
