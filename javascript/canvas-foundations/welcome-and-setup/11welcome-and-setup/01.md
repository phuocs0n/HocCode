## What You'll Build

Every concept in this course leads toward one goal. You are going to build Brick Breaker. If you have never played it, here is how it works.

You have a paddle. The ball is in play. Above you, rows of bricks are waiting.

- Every time the ball hits a brick, it vanishes
- Miss the ball and the game is over
- Clear every brick and you win

Simple rules. Surprisingly addictive.

Next, let's look at exactly what you will learn to make this happen.
