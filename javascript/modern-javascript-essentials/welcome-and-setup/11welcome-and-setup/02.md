## Why Learn Modern JavaScript?

You might be thinking: "Can't I just use regular JavaScript?"

You can, but Modern JavaScript makes your code:

- Easier to read - Clear, expressive syntax that communicates intent
- Less buggy - Features like const and optional chaining prevent common mistakes
- More maintainable - Better organization with classes and clear structure
- Industry standard - What professional teams expect in 2026

When you apply for developer positions or collaborate on projects, Modern JavaScript is the baseline. It's not advanced - it's foundational.
