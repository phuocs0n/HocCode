## Broadcastable Shapes

Here are examples of the shapes that are broadcastable:

- (6, 7) and (6, 7)

Since they have the same shape, they are compatible with each other.

Resulting Shape: (6, 7)

- (6, 7) and (6, 1)

Comparing from the right dimensions, 7 and 1 are compatible, 6 and 6 are compatible. Hence, these are broadcastable.

Resulting Shape: (6, 7)

- (4, 3) and (3, )

Here, we pad the second shape (3, ) with 1 on the left making the shape: (1, 3).

Now the shapes are (4, 3) and (1, 3).

Comparing dimensions from right: 3 and 3 are compatible, 1 and 4 are compatible.

Resulting Shape: (4, 3)

Non-Broadcastable Shapes

- (6, 7) and (7, 6)

The right dimensions (7 and 6) are not equal and neither is 1. So, these shapes are not compatible.

- (6, 7) and (6, )

These shapes are not broadcastable because (6, ) when padded with one we get (1, 6) which is not compatible with (6,7).
