## Thought Process

To solve any SQL query, it's essential to first understand what the question is asking.

Understanding the problem allows us to approach the problem correctly and figure out an efficient solution.

As you can tell, we are given a table named EPL with columns for position, team, goals, and points.

Our task involves writing an SQL query to retrieve the teams in the top 4 positions with more than 65 points and more than 50 goals from the EPL table.

Now, let's move on to how to approach this question.

Start by selecting the team column from the EPL table. Then, filter the teams based on the following conditions:

- Choose teams within the top 4 positions.
- Ensure that these teams have achieved more than 65 points.
- Confirm that they have scored more than 50 goals.
