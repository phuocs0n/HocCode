## Thought Process

We're given an Employees table with columns emp_id, name, department, and salary.

We need to check two conditions here: either the employees belong to the Sales department, or they have a salary greater than 50000.

Furthermore, the Sales employees cannot have salaries greater than 50000. We must use a WHERE clause in our SQL query to check for these conditions.

For the first condition, we will check if the employee's department is Sales and their salary is less than or equal to 50,000.

For the second condition, we will check for employees that don't belong to the Sales department with a salary greater than 50,000.

We must use the logical OR operator to combine these conditions, as only one must be true for selecting an employee.
