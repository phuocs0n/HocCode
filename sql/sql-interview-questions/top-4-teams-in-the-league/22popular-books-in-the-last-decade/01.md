## Thought Process

We are given a table named Books with columns for book_id, title, date, and sales.

The goal is to write an SQL query that retrieves all books published in the last decade (2010 - 2019) which has sold more than 1 million copies, along with their sales numbers.

Now, let's move on to how to approach this problem.

Start by selecting the title and sales columns from the Books table. Then, filter the books based on the following conditions:

- The publication date is between January 1, 2010, and December 31, 2019.
- The sales exceed 1 million copies.
