## Thought Process

The Anime table in our problem has the columns id, title, rating, and seasons.

The problem statement requires us to retrieve the names of all anime titles with seasons not equal to 20 and ratings higher than the average rating of the table.

So, to solve this problem, we need to check for two things:

- Seasons that are not equal to 20 using the not equal to <> operator.
- Rating greater than the average rating of the table with the AVG() function.

It may seem easy to solve, but the caveat is using the AVG() function correctly.

We must structure this query as a subquery and use the AVG() function inside the nested query.
