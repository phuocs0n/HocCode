## Question

Consider the following tables:

Table - Orders

| order_id | customer_id | order_date |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1001 | 2023-04-15 |
| 2 | 1002 | 2023-05-10 |
| 3 | 1003 | 2023-05-01 |
| 4 | 1004 | 2023-04-25 |
| 5 | 1005 | 2023-05-15 |

Table - Items

| order_id | product_id | quantity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 2001 | 5 |
| 1 | 2002 | 3 |
| 2 | 2003 | 2 |
| 2 | 2004 | 4 |
| 2 | 2005 | 1 |
| 3 | 2001 | 2 |
| 4 | 2002 | 3 |
| 5 | 2003 | 6 |

Write an SQL query to display the total quantity of products ordered by each customer. Retrieve the customer ID and the total quantity of products grouped by each customer.

Hint: Use INNER JOIN.
