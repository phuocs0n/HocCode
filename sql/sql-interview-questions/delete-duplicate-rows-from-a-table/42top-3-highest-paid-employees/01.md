## Thought Process

We are given a table named Employees with columns emp_id, name, department, salary, type, and experience.

We are required to retrieve the names of the top 3 highest-paid full-time employees in the Sales department with a salary greater than $5,000 and experience greater than or equal to 5 years.

Finally, we need to sort them by their salary in descending order.

At first glance, the above problem might seem simple as we just need to use a bunch of conditions in the WHERE clause. However, there is one tricky part about the problem.

For this problem, we need to use the LIMIT 3 statement to filter the top 3 results. LIMIT 3 means that we can filter a maximum of 3 rows.

Our query needs to select the names of employees who belong to the Sales department with a salary exceeding 5000 and experience greater than or equal to 5.

We then need to sort the results in descending order of salary and limit to only three results

The query will result in the following table:

| name |
| --- |
| Paul |
| Rey |

As you can see, our result only filters 2 names because they are the only employees who meet all the conditions.
