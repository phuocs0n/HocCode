#### Course: Learn SQL Basics

## Finding Average Product Price

The average price of your products provides an overview of your pricing strategy, helping to refine it and assess competitiveness in the market.

To find this average, simply use the AVG() function.

```sql
1SELECT AVG(price) AS average_price
2FROM Products;
```

Insights:

- Average price: $181.67

This insight shows that your products are priced moderately, making them appealing to mid-range buyers.
