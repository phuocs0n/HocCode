## Finding Unique Brands

The variety of brands you carry can help you determine whether your inventory is dominated by a few brands or spread across many.

To find this, you use the COUNT() function with DISTINCT:

```sql
1SELECT COUNT(DISTINCT brand) AS unique_brands
2FROM Products;
```

Insights:

- Unique brands: 4

This insight shows that your products come from ByteCore, ZapTech, ViewPlus, and Clickio, giving customers multiple options.
