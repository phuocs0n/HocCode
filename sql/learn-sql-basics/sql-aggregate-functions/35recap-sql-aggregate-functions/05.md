## Finding Total Products

The total number of products lets you gauge the diversity of your inventory. It also helps track growth as you add more items to your catalog.

```sql
1SELECT COUNT(*) AS total_products
2FROM Products;
```

Insights:

- Total products: 6

This insight indicates that your current offerings are limited, which may suggest expanding your product range.
