## Example 2: Customers Without Purchases

To identify customers who have not made any purchases, you need to filter out the customers with no linked orders.

You can use a LEFT JOIN between the Customers and Orders tables and then apply a WHERE clause to check for NULL in the Orders.product_name column.

```sql
1-- Use LEFT JOIN to list all customers and 
2-- Identify those without purchases
3
4SELECT Customers.customer_name, Customers.city
5FROM Customers
6LEFT JOIN Orders ON Customers.customer_id = Orders.customer_id
7WHERE Orders.product_name IS NULL;
```
