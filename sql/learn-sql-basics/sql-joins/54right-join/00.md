## Introduction to RIGHT JOIN

Previously, you used LEFT JOIN to retrieve all records from the left table, such as listing all customers, even those without orders.

But what if your focus is on the orders?

For example, you want to analyze all orders placed, including those that don't have a corresponding customer record—perhaps due to missing customer data or incomplete entries.

To do this, you can use RIGHT JOIN. Let's explore how on the next page.
