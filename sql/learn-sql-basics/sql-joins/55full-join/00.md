## Introduction to FULL JOIN

Previously, you used LEFT JOIN and RIGHT JOIN to retrieve records from one table while including unmatched rows from either the left or right table.

But what if you need to analyze all data from both tables?

For example, you might want to identify all customers, their orders, and any unmatched records—such as customers without orders or orders missing associated customer details.

For this, you can use a FULL JOIN. Let's explore FULL JOIN on the next page.
