## Example 2: Orders by New York Customers

Now that you've learned how to join tables and select specific columns, let's take it a step further.

What if you want to filter the results of your join? For example, instead of seeing all customers and their purchases, you only want to see customers from New York and the products they bought.

For this, you can use the WHERE clause after joining tables. For example,

```sql
1-- Join the Customers and Orders table
2-- Return all the columns
3-- Select rows if the customer is from New York
4
5SELECT Customers.customer_name, Customers.city, Orders.product_name
6FROM Customers
7INNER JOIN Orders
8ON Customers.customer_id = Orders.customer_id
9WHERE Customers.city = 'New York';
```

You can see customers from New York and the products they purchase.
