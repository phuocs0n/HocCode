## Finding the Salary Range in Each Department

You are asked to find the minimum and maximum salary in each department to understand the distribution of salaries among employees.

To accomplish this, use GROUP BY with MIN() and MAX() aggregate functions.

```sql
1-- Find the salary range in each department
2
3SELECT department, MIN(salary) AS minimum_salary, 
4MAX(salary) AS maximum_salary
5FROM Employees
6GROUP BY department;
```
