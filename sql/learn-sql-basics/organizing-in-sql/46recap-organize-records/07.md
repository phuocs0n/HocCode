## Finding Employees Eligible for Promotion

The company is preparing to promote its employees based on their years of service. Consequently, your manager asks you to categorize employees accordingly.

The idea behind this is:

- Years of Service >= 10: Eligible for an Executive Role
- Years of Service >= 5: Eligible for Senior-Level Promotion
- Years of Service >= 3: Eligible for Mid-Level Promotion
- Years of Service < 3: Not Eligible for Promotion

To do this, use SQL CASE as follows:

```sql
1-- Identify employees eligible for Promotion
2
3SELECT 
4    id,
5    name,
6    years_of_service,
7    CASE 
8        WHEN years_of_service >= 10 THEN 'Eligible for Executive Role'
9        WHEN years_of_service >= 5 THEN 'Eligible for Senior-Level Promotion'
10        WHEN years_of_service >= 3 THEN 'Eligible for Mid-Level Promotion'
11        ELSE 'Not Eligible for Promotion'
12    END AS promotion_eligibility
13FROM Employees;
```

These data can help the company build a strong leadership pipeline and maintain a future-ready workforce.
