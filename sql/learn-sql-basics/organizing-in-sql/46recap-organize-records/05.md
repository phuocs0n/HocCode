## Finding the Understaffed Departments

To gain deeper insights, you're asked to find the departments with less than two employees. This helps the company pinpoint understaffed departments.

For this, use the HAVING clause to filter the results after applying the GROUP BY clause.

```sql
1-- Find department with less than 2 employees
2
3SELECT department, COUNT(*) AS num_of_employees
4FROM Employees
5GROUP BY department
6HAVING COUNT(*) < 2;
```

From these insights, the company can increase its workforce depending on its requirements.
