## Check Leap Year (III)

You'll often find more than one approach to solve a problem when programming. Let's look at another approach:

```c
1#include <stdio.h>
2
3int main() {
4
5  int year = 2000;
6
7  // If a year is divisible by 4 and not divisible by 100
8  // Or year is divisible by 400, it's a leap year
9  if ((year % 4 == 0 && year % 100 != 0) || year % 400 == 0) {
10    printf("Leap Year");
11  }
12
13  // All other remaining years are not a leap year
14  else {
15    printf("Not a Leap Year");
16  }
17
18return 0;
19}
```

Using the && (AND) and || (OR) operator, you can check all three conditions at once.
