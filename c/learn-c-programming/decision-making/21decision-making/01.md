## Decision Making in Programming

We are naturally aware of the idea of true and false in decision making.

For instance, when someone says, "The sun rises in the east," you'll immediately think of it as true.

But, if someone says, "1 + 1 = 3," you might think it makes no sense and is false.

Our brain works by turning the sentence into a question to get a true/false answer.

Does the sun rise in the east? True

Is 1 + 1 equal to 3? False

Computers follow logic when making decisions in a similar way.
