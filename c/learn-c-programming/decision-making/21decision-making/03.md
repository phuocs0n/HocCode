## Boolean Expressions (I)

Imagine you're helping a coffee shop automate its billing system. They have a special offer: Spend more than $100 and get a free dessert.

Previously, to determine whether a customer qualifies for the dessert, the cashier used to manually check, "Is the total amount greater than $100?"

Now, the billing system has to handle this decision-making.

Let's say the total amount spent by a customer is $70.

To check if they qualify for the dessert, we can express this condition as:

```c
total > 100
```

In this case, since $70 is not greater than $100, the condition evaluates to false, which in C is represented as 0. Therefore, the customer does not get a dessert.
