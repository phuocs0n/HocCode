# Why I Wrote this Course

#### Course: HTML Fundamentals for Your First Webpage

## Why I Wrote this Course

I still remember my first experience learning HTML. Back then, it felt like an overwhelming list of syntaxes that I could never hope to remember.

But over time, HTML started to feel like the building blocks of a page, giving me the tools to lay down a solid structure on the blank page of the browser.

That's when I realized that grasping the fundamentals was all I really needed to start building real webpages.

Looking back, I know it could have been taught in a much more intuitive way. This course is my attempt to bring you just that.
