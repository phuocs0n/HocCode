# My Teaching Philosophy

#### Course: HTML Fundamentals for Your First Webpage

## My Teaching Philosophy

As an instructor, my main challenge is to provide you with an intuitive learning approach.

And the solution, in my opinion, is to give you just enough HTML to start creating functional webpages. The course should progress logically and systematically, in much the same way I'd train an aspiring junior developer who's stepping into web development.

That's my core teaching philosophy:

- Practical
- Minimalistic
- To the point
