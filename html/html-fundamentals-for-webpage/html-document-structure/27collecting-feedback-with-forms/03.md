## Example: A Simple Blog Feedback Form

Here's a basic feedback form for our Learning HTML blog:

```html
1<form>
2  <label>
3    Your Name:
4    <input type="text">
5  </label>
6
7  <label>
8    Your Feedback:
9    <textarea></textarea>
10  </label>
11
12  <button type="submit">Send Feedback</button>
13</form>
```

Here's what's happening:

1. <form> groups everything
2. <label> explains each input
3. <input> collects short text
4. <textarea> collects longer messages
5. <button> submits the form

This already works — even without styling.
