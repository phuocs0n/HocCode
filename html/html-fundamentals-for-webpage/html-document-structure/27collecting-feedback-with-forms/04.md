## Developer Insight: A Small But Important Detail

Notice how the text and input are grouped together inside <label>.

This helps:

1. Screen readers
2. Click behavior
3. Clarity for users

You may not see any visual change if you decouple <label> and input fields like this:

```html
1<form>
2  <label>
3    Your Name:</label>
4  <input type="text">
5
6  <label>
7    Your Feedback:
8  </label>
9  <textarea></textarea>
10
11  <button type="submit">Send Feedback</button>
12</form>
```

So, it's valid to ask why you should bother grouping the tags together.

But trust me, you'll realize the importance of this practice once you learn JavaScript. For now, just remember that it's much better to group them together.
