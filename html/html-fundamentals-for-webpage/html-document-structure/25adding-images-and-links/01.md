#### Course: HTML Fundamentals for Your First Webpage

## Lesson Goal

You'll now learn to add images and hyperlinks to your webpage.

Images add visual information to your website, while links can take you to other webpages or other online resources.

Here are the HTML tags you'll learn:

- <img> - Adds images to your webpage. Here, img stands for image.
- <a> - Adds links to your webpage. Here, a stands for anchor, i.e., it anchors another page or resource to your webpage.
