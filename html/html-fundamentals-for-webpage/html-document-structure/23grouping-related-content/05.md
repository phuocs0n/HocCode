## Using Grouping in Our Blog

Now let's apply grouping to the Learning HTML blog.

```html
1<section>
2  <h2>Why Learn HTML?</h2>
3  <p>
4    HTML is the foundation of every webpage. Understanding it makes learning
5    other technologies easier.
6  </p>
7</section>
8
9<section>
10  <h2>Who This Blog Is For</h2>
11  <p>
12    This blog is for beginners who want to understand how the web works,
13    step by step.
14  </p>
15</section>
```

Here, each section:

1. Covers one idea
2. Keeps content focused
3. Makes the page easier to scan
