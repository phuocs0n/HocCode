## Why Grouping Content Matters

If you only use headings and paragraphs, your page still works; but it quickly becomes harder to read and maintain.

Real developers pause and ask:

- Which content belongs together?
- Where does one idea end and the next begin?

Grouping helps answer these questions and keeps content clear.
