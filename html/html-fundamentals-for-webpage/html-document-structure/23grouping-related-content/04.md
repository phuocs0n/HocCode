## The span Tag: Grouping Inline Content

Sometimes you don't want to group a block — just a small part of text.

That's where <span> is used.

- <span> groups inline content
- It does not create a new block
- It's often used to highlight or target part of a sentence

Inline vs Block Elements

You might be wondering what inline content means.

An inline element doesn't take an entire line or block of space in your page; they only take the space that they need. So, other elements can exist in the same line as an inline element.

Block elements are the exact opposite, i.e., they take an entire line of space.

Example

```html
1<p>
2  HTML is the <span>foundation</span> of every webpage.
3</p>
```

Think of <span> as the inline version of <div>.
