## Section 1: What You'll Learn

This section of the blog is designed to teach how lists are created in HTML. The section itself is about what your blog readers will learn.

We have chosen lists to make such content clearer than long paragraphs.

```html
1<section>
2  <h2>What You'll Learn</h2>
3  <ul>
4    <li>How HTML structures content</li>
5    <li>How to group related elements</li>
6    <li>How to build readable webpages</li>
7  </ul>
8</section>
```

Why this works:

- <section> - tackles only one idea, i.e., what readers will learn in the blog
- <h2> - specifies the topic of the section
- <ul> - groups the related points, i.e., the individual topics readers will learn
