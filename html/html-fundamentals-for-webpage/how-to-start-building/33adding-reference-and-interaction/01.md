## Why Blogs Need More Than Text

At this point, your blog explains concepts well. But real blogs usually do two more things:

- Present structured information for reference
- Let readers interact or respond

As you know, developers solve these with:

- Tables for structured data
- Forms for user input

And as it turns out, our remaining sections (HTML Tag Reference and Feedback) are meant to do just that: present structured information and get reader response.

So we'll add both in this lesson.
