# Why is <title> important?

#### Course: Learn HTML

## Why is <title> important?

Search engines like Google use the <title> tag to understand the topic of a page. This helps them display relevant websites to users.

That's why it's important to follow best practices while writing the title.

Best Practices

- Use a single <title> element per webpage.
- Write a title that accurately reflects the page's content.
- Use a concise yet descriptive title.

Example

Good Title - "HTML form Elements"

Bad Title - "Form"

The first title provides more context and is likely to perform better in search results.
