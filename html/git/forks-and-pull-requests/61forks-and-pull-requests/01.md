## Why Pull Requests Exist

Without pull requests, anyone with access could push changes directly to main. Changes would go in without review, and mistakes could easily slip through.

A pull request adds an important step. Before anything is merged, someone reviews the changes.

This catches most mistakes and improves quality. It also creates a clear record of what changed, why it changed, and who approved it.
